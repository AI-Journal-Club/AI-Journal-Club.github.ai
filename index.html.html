AI Opportunities in Science: Partnerships, Interdisciplinary Research and Education

A one-and-a-half-day workshop bringing together researchers at every career stage across LSU College of Science departments — and interested colleagues from Computer Science, CCT, and the College of Education — active or interested in AI/ML-related research.

Register interest View schedule
Synopsis

This workshop aims to bring together researchers at every career stage, active or interested in pursuing AI/ML-related research across departments of the LSU College of Science. Participation may be extended to specific departments of other colleges — e.g. Computer Science, CCT, and the College of Education.

Organizers

LSU AI Journal Club as the primary driving force, with volunteers from each department and interested parties.

Why this workshop
01

Partnerships

Connecting university researchers with national labs, industry, and funding agencies to scale AI-driven science beyond any one lab.

02

Interdisciplinary Research

Pairing domain scientists with AI researchers to tackle problems in physics, biology, and earth science that neither could solve alone.

03

Education

Building coursework, training, and mentorship that prepares the next generation of scientists to use AI fluently and responsibly.

Workshop topics

Sessions and discussions will span:

Foundation Models for Scientific Discovery

AI for Physics & Astronomy

AI for Biology, Chemistry & Materials

Scientific Machine Learning & Simulation

Responsible & Trustworthy AI

Data, Compute & Research Infrastructure

Funding Models for AI–Science Partnerships

AI Literacy & Curriculum Design

Interdisciplinary AI–science research is a partnership problem as much as a technical one.

This workshop exists to build the relationships — across departments, institutions, and funding agencies — that make that research possible.
